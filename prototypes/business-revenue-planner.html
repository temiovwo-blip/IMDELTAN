How to use this safely

Gross is businesses × price. Refunds and percentage fees are calculated on gross; the fixed payment fee is charged once per business. The amount left subtracts those deductions and the three monthly cost inputs.

Taxes, chargebacks, per-refund fees, annual expenses, unpaid customers, price changes and many real-world costs are not automatically included. Use your own researched estimates and professional advice before making financial decisions. No outcome or income is guaranteed.

Privacy: this calculator runs in your browser and does not save or send your entries. It is a planning prototype, not part of the installed app.
