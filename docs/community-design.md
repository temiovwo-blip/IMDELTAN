# Delta communities and groups

Updated 2026-11-06, following the owner's decision: the app has 25 **LGA communities**, one for each Delta State local government area. **Groups** are smaller spaces created inside an LGA community, for example a football group in Uvwie. Groups and LGA communities are distinct: a group belongs to one LGA community, not the other way around.

During account setup, a person selects their local government. Their profile is intended to display only that LGA name, such as “Uvwie” or “Okpe”: do not prepend “Proud” and do not infer or collect ethnic identity from the choice. Isoko North and Isoko South are separate LGAs. Visibility of the LGA to other users, joining other communities, group discovery, approval, invitations, moderation, ownership transfers and LGA changes' effect on membership remain decisions to resolve; use privacy-preserving defaults and do not invent access rules.

Draft migration 026 seeds the 25 names, establishes one LGA-community row per seeded LGA and a default-deny group table, and adds self-only LGA setup RPCs and an activation gate. Migration 027 adds draft private group creation in the creator’s selected LGA and creates an owner membership atomically. Migration 029 drafts owner-sent invitations and accept/decline with same-LGA and block checks; same-LGA restriction is a conservative temporary assumption, not a settled product decision. Migration 031 drafts an exact-username owner invitation search using public-profile and contact settings; there is no broad member discovery. Public profiles, actual LGA membership, group posts, full abuse controls and cross-community navigation are not implemented. The previous generic `communities` schema is a separate earlier draft and must be reconciled with this product direction before content features are exposed. No new data has been deployed to the development project. The LGA list should be verified against an authoritative Delta State or Nigerian government source before production; development references include https://www.jobzilla.ng/article/local-government-areas-lgas-in-delta-state-784.
