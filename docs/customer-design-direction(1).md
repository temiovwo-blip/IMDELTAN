# IMDELTAN customer design direction

Status: visual direction approved by owner; prototype only, not a working application.

## Identity

Use a clean white interface with light-green accents and darker green for important text and controls. Keep the layout welcoming and uncluttered. The green colour choice is approved; specific shades require contrast testing and may be adjusted for readability. The design must work on phones and adapt to wider screens.

## Home and navigation

The home view prioritizes community discovery, a mixed feed of text and media, and clear audience labels. Planned primary navigation: Home, Discover, Create, Communities and Messages. Profile/settings and notifications remain easy to find. Labels accompany icons. Existing prototype interactions are demonstrations, not implemented product features.

## Ethical engagement

Encourage belonging through useful community conversations and relevant stories, not fake urgency or deceptive engagement. Do not invent member counts or activity. Provide natural stopping points and user control. Video should be tap-to-play by default; make data-saving controls available when implemented.

## Accessibility and privacy

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the customer web experience; test contrast, screen readers, focus, keyboard access, scaling and touch targets before launch. The prototype is not certified. Show post audience clearly; reporting, blocking and message permissions must be accessible and enforced on the server. Optional ethnic group information must be private by default and subject to reviewed consent and withdrawal workflows.

## Media and placeholder content

The prototype includes an AI-generated illustration and fictitious example content. They do not represent real people, members or statistics. Production assets require appropriate rights and review. Optimize images, load only what is needed and support slow networks.

## Boundaries and release

The prototype has no backend, real authentication, uploads, messages, moderation or saved data. It has not been deployed or pushed from this workspace. Do not open registration until legal and security approval. Android remains a separate application with a new identity and signing arrangement.
